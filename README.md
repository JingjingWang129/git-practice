## An Article I Found Interesting

[10 things I learned from burning myself out with AI coding agents](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/01/10-things-i-learned-from-burning-myself-out-with-ai-coding-agents/) — Benj Edwards, *Ars Technica*

### My Thoughts

Benj used different AI agents to build many hobby projects, covering a wide range of fields and topics. This gives people who aren't familiar with AI a lot of confidence. It shows them that simply describing an idea and doing a bit of typing can produce something small but genuinely useful for themselves.

At the same time, it also suggests that veteran programmers don't need to worry excessively about being replaced. Building a mature, production-ready product still requires the added value of experience and skill to develop well.
